# Tamagoshi Outdoor: Less Screen. More World.

> Draft only. Replace placeholders with actual evidence. Check the official DEV template before submission.

## What I built
Tamagoshi Outdoor is a friendly open-model companion for adults who spend long days at their desks. It suggests tiny, safe outdoor adventures and celebrates real-world discoveries.

## Why it matters
Instead of encouraging endless engagement, the app asks people to close their screens and step outside.

## How open AI is used
A locally hosted Gemma model (via Ollama) generates three personalized missions based on time and interests. Its JSON output is validated by the backend. If the model is unavailable, clearly labeled demo missions are used.

## Stack
React/Vite, Python/FastAPI, SQLite, Ollama + Gemma.
